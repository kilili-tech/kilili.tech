    Questions

    Does KILILI really work with no data?

    Yes. Chats, calls, photos, files, rooms, events and games with people near you go phone to phone over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. No data bundle, no airtime, no signal. Friends further away get your message when phones in between carry it.

    How do people find me?

    People near you see you at the top of their Chats. You can also add your phone number (optional) so friends can invite you by number; it isn't shown to everyone.

    Is it safe and private?

    Your account lives on your phone, not on a company server. Messages are locked so only the person you send them to can read them. No ads, no tracking.

    What if I lose my phone?

    KILILI saves your account file to Downloads/KILILI every day. On a new phone, tap Sign in, choose the file and type your password.
