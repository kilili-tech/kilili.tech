Step-by-step install help (Android warnings explained)
  1. Tap Download KILILI. If Chrome says "This type of file can harm your device", tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the file from the notification, or from Files → Downloads → KILILI.apk.
  3. If Android says it is "not allowed to install unknown apps from this source", tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then press Back.
  4. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns you, tap More details → Install anyway.
  5. Tap Open and allow Nearby devices, Location and Notifications.

Android shows these warnings for every app installed from outside Play Store. KILILI is signed with its own permanent key; you can check your copy below in "Keep your account safe".

    Questions

    What is KILILI?

    KILILI is an app for chatting, sharing files, making calls, posting stories, playing games and connecting with people nearby, phone to phone.

    Does KILILI require mobile data?

    No. Chat, calls, file sharing, stories, rooms, events and games with people nearby work over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi between phones, with no mobile data or internet. Messages to friends further away hop through other KILILI phones, so they arrive when a path of phones exists. Buying a pass on this website uses the internet.

    How do people find me on KILILI?

    People in range see you at the top of their Chats screen. You can also add your phone number (optional) so friends can invite you by number: the invite goes from your own SMS app, and your number is not published for everyone.

    Can I find people nearby?

    Yes. Install KILILI, create your account, turn on Bluetooth and Wi-Fi and allow the permissions. People near you appear under Around you now at the top of Chats.

    Can I share large files?

    Yes, with phones near you. Turn on Wi-Fi on both phones for the fastest transfers.

    Does KILILI work on iPhone?

    Not yet. KILILI runs on Android 8 and newer; the iPhone version is coming.

    What happens if I lose my phone?

    Install KILILI on the new phone, tap I already have an account, choose your account file and type your recovery password. Without the file and password the account cannot be recovered, because no company holds a copy.

    Already have an old KILILI test version?

    In the old app, save your account file (Me → Back up or move to a new phone). Uninstall it, install this one, tap I already have an account and pick the file from Downloads/KILILI.
